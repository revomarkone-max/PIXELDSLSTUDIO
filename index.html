North (Back View) Inversion

When character faces North, layers are automatically sorted in reverse:
Z_{new} = Z_{max} - Z_{old}
Capes and backpacks shift to foreground (Z=2), while facial features and chests are culled or occluded.

Isometric 2.5D Projection

Applies standard 30° axonometric transform:
X_{screen} = (X - Y) × cos(30°)
Y_{screen} = (X + Y) × sin(30°) - Z
